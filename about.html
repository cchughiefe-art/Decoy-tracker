About Lumina

Lumina Tools started as a small collection of scripts we kept rewriting for our own projects. We turned them into simple web utilities so anyone can use them without installing anything.

Our approach

Keep tools focused. Prefer client-side processing. Avoid accounts and unnecessary data collection. Ship useful things quickly.

Who it’s for

Developers, designers, writers, students — anyone who needs a quick formatter, converter, or generator without jumping through hoops.

Roadmap

A few things currently in progress:

  • More developer utilities (JWT decoder, regex tester, cron explainer)
  • Lightweight image tools (compress, resize, convert)
  • Better mobile experience across all tools
  • Optional dark / light theme toggle
